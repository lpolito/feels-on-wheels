# Danckert and others: boredom is an emotion

Why Bored is on the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md).

## What it is

- James Danckert, Jhotisha Mugon, Andriy Struk and John Eastwood, "Boredom: What is it good for?", in Heather C. Lench (ed.), *The Function of Emotions*, Springer, 2018. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-77619-4_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-77619-4_6).
- **The idea:** boredom is a "ubiquitous and consequential human emotion" that signals a need to change what you're doing.
- The chapter was read in full during the opinionated wheel's research.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Bored is a feeling,** and it goes under Bad: people who are bored don't report sadness or fear along with it.
