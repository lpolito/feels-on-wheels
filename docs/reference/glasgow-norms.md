# The Glasgow Norms: words with more than one meaning

Used during the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md)'s research to tell a word's meanings apart.

## What it is

- Graham G. Scott, Anne Keitel, Marc Becirspahic, Bo Yao and Sara C. Sereno, "The Glasgow Norms: Ratings of 5,500 words on nine scales", *Behavior Research Methods* 51(3), 1258–1270, 2019. [doi:10.3758/s13428-018-1099-3](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-018-1099-3).
- **What it is:** 5,553 English words rated on nine scales by the same raters, with 379 ambiguous words split by meaning ("content (happy)").
- Downloaded and used directly during the research (2026-11-19 to 2026-11-21). No script reads it now.
