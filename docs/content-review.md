# v1 content review

One entry per word. A word in two places on the wheel is listed once, where it first appears. Edit freely: definitions live in `src/data/locales/en.json`, and each feeling's sensations in `src/data/feelings.json`. The italic lines are what a feeling's card shows under "You might notice" (`en.json` → `sensationPhrases`); the wheel shows shorter labels (`en.json` → `sensations`).

## Angry

- **Angry**: Heated and wanting to push back at someone or something that bothers you, wrongs you, or gets in your way.  
  _A hot face · Clenched teeth · Tight fists · A racing heart_
  - **Offended**: Angry or upset at words or actions that feel disrespectful.  
    _A hot face · Clenched teeth · A tight chest_
    - **Indignant**: Angry at being wronged or disrespected, and sure you're in the right.  
      _A hot face · Tense shoulders · Clenched teeth_
    - **Insulted**: Stung that someone seems to have a low opinion of you or of what you care about.  
      _A hot face · Clenched teeth · Wanting to lash out_
  - **Annoyed**: Mildly irritated by something that bothers you.  
    _Tense shoulders · Feeling restless · Clenched teeth_
    - **Provoked**: Annoyed by someone who's trying to get a reaction out of you.  
      _Wanting to lash out · A hot face · Clenched teeth · A racing heart_
    - **Irritable**: Short-tempered, as if every little thing bothers you.  
      _Tense shoulders · Clenched teeth · Wanting to be alone_
    - **Impatient**: Wanting something to hurry up, and annoyed at the wait.  
      _Feeling restless · Tense shoulders_
  - **Frustrated**: Upset because something keeps getting in your way.  
    _A headache · Tense shoulders · Tight fists · Wanting to cry_
    - **Fed up**: Out of patience with the same thing happening over and over.  
      _Clenched teeth · A headache · Tense shoulders · Wanting to give up_
    - **Trapped**: Frustrated and stuck, like there's no way out.  
      _A tight chest · Wanting to run · Short, shallow breaths_
  - **Furious**: Extremely angry, and hard to calm down.  
    _Shaky hands · Wanting to lash out · A hot face · Tight fists_
    - **Rage**: Anger so strong that it takes over.  
      _A hot face · Shaky hands · Wanting to lash out · Tight fists_
    - **Violated**: Furious that someone invaded something that's yours alone, like your body, privacy, or home.  
      _Your skin crawling · A knot in your stomach · Feeling queasy · Feeling frozen_
    - **Outraged**: Furious at something wrong or unjust.  
      _A hot face · Wanting to lash out · A racing heart_
  - **Disgusted**: Sickened by something cruel or morally wrong.  
    _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away_
    - **Appalled**: Shocked and in disbelief that anyone could do something so wrong.  
      _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away · Feeling frozen_
    - **Contempt**: Cold disgust for someone or something you have no respect for.  
      _Wanting to back away · Feeling tall_
    - **Hatred**: Intense, lasting hostility toward someone or something, for who they are or what they stand for.  
      _Clenched teeth · A tight chest · Wanting to lash out_
  - **Resentful**: Holding on to anger about being treated unfairly.  
    _Clenched teeth · A tight chest · Wanting to lash out · Tense shoulders_
    - **Betrayed**: Hurt and angry because someone you trusted broke that trust.  
      _A knot in your stomach · Wanting to lash out · A hollow feeling in your chest · A lump in your throat_
    - **Used**: Resentful that someone took advantage of you to get what they wanted.  
      _A knot in your stomach · Clenched teeth · A hollow feeling in your chest_
    - **Cheated**: Angry that someone tricked you or didn't play fair.  
      _A knot in your stomach · Clenched teeth · A hot face_
  - **Jealous**: Angry that someone has what you want, or could take away someone or something you care about.  
    _Wanting to control things · A knot in your stomach · A tight chest · A hot face_
    - **Envious**: Wanting what someone else has, and bothered that it's theirs.  
      _A knot in your stomach · A tight chest_
    - **Possessive**: Wanting to keep someone or something to yourself, and wary of anyone who comes near them.  
      _Wanting to control things · Wanting to be close · Tense shoulders_

  _A racing heart · Shaky hands · Feeling frozen · Wanting to run_

    - **Insecure**: Unsure whether you're good enough, or whether people like you.

## Body wheel

The body sensations wheel after the [body wheel rework](superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-design.md) (with its [changes](superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-changes.md)), repaired to the [opinionated feelings wheel](superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md#the-body-wheel)'s words: body areas, the sensations under each, and the 2–3 feelings each sensation leads to. These are drafted to agree with each feeling's "You might notice" above, though nothing enforces it. The wheel shows the short labels below; cards show the phrases above.

### Head / face

- **Headache**: Frustrated, Overwhelmed
- **Foggy head**: Confused, Down, Burned out
- **Lightheaded**: Shocked, Overwhelmed, Nervous
- **Hot face**: Embarrassed, Ashamed

- **Heart in throat**: Terrified, Nervous, Startled

- **Tight throat**: Anxious, Panicked, Disgusted
- **Dry mouth**: Nervous, Terrified
- **Clenched teeth**: Frustrated, Overwhelmed, Resentful

### Shoulders

- **Tense shoulders**: Anxious, Frustrated, Overwhelmed
- **Weight on shoulders**: Helpless, Pressured

### Hands

- **Tight fists**: Furious, Frustrated
- **Shaky hands**: Scared, Excited, Furious
- **Sweaty palms**: Nervous, Excited

### Chest
