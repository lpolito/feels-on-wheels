# Sara Algoe: gratitude

Where Grateful sits on the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md): first placed under Love for what gratitude does, then moved to Happy for how it feels.

## What it is

- Sara B. Algoe, "Find, remind, and bind: The functions of gratitude in everyday relationships", *Social and Personality Psychology Compass* 6(6), 455–469, 2012. [doi:10.1111/j.1751-9004.2012.00439.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-9004.2012.00439.x): gratitude helps people find, remind themselves of, and bind to good relationships.
- Sara B. Algoe and Jonathan Haidt, "Witnessing excellence in action: The 'other-praising' emotions of elevation, gratitude, and admiration", *The Journal of Positive Psychology* 4(2), 105–127, 2009. [doi:10.1080/17439760802650519](https://doi.org/10.1080/17439760802650519): asked for the best single word for remembered gratitude, 42% said "happiness" and 29% "gratitude".
- The 2009 paper was read in full; the 2012 paper's idea comes from its abstract.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.
