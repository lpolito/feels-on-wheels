# Changes from the spec

Where *Keys turn the wheel, quiet focus after a tap, real links, and tap to close* as shipped differs from its approved [spec](2026-11-14-feels-on-wheels-keys-and-focus-design.md). The spec stays as it was approved.

- **A double tap on the opened word closes it** (Lucas, 2026-11-14, using the preview). The spec had the double tap close it and open it again, so it stayed open. Lucas double-clicks the opened word to close it. Now the first tap closes it and the second does nothing more.
- **A quick push never merges into a copy of the entry before it** (Lucas, 2026-11-14, using the preview). This is a change to the [history rule](2026-11-14-feels-on-wheels-history-rule-design.md)'s "pushes closer together than 250 ms merge". Two pushes within 250 ms that come back to where they started merged into a copy of the entry before them, so the same address appeared twice in a row and Back seemed not to work. That happens when you open a word and close it at once (Enter twice on the card's toggle, or a double tap on the opened word as the spec first had it), or double-click a card word so the second click lands on the breadcrumb that slides in for the word you came from. Now a push that would make the entry the same as the one before it adds its own entry instead. Each click gets its own entry, and Back steps through them.
- **Only a spin makes a tap "stop here"** (Lucas, 2026-11-14, using the preview: a tap on the selected word sometimes didn't open it, but a second one did). The spec kept the rule that a tap on a moving wheel only stops it. "Moving" included the app's own moves, not only a spin: the zoom out after closing, the zoom in after opening, and the arrow keys' turn. So a tap on the word within about half a second of one of those only stopped the camera, sometimes part-way zoomed. Now a tap is "stop here" only while the wheel is still heading where your own fling sent it (v1.1's intent: catching a spinning wheel). During the app's own moves, a tap opens, closes or selects at once, and the move carries on to wherever that sends it.
