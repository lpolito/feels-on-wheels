# Baron-Cohen and others: which feeling words children know

Why the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md)'s cores are words young children know.

## What it is

- Simon Baron-Cohen, Ofer Golan, Sally Wheelwright, Yael Granader and Jacqueline Hill, "Emotion word comprehension from 4 to 16 years old: A developmental survey", *Frontiers in Evolutionary Neuroscience* 2, 109, 2010. [doi:10.3389/fnevo.2010.00109](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnevo.2010.00109). Open access.
- **What it is:** for 336 emotion words, the share of children in six age bands, from 4–6 to 15–16, who understood each one; under 12, by parent or teacher report. 377 children in Cambridgeshire, UK.
- **Its cut-off,** 75% of a band, was chosen as above chance, not as "knows it well". Some bands are small, and Bad isn't on its list.
- Read in full, and its table used directly by the local scripts.

## Usage terms

Open access, with credit.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **A core's label** is a word at least 75% of 4- to 6-year-olds knew, where the list has it: Scared over Fearful.
2. **The kids' wheels** can use it to choose words for each age.
