# DSM-5: the body symptoms of panic, anxiety and depression

The standard lists of what panic, anxiety and depression feel like, used to back many of the sensations in the [body wheel rework](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-design.md).

## What it is

- American Psychiatric Association, *Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders*, 5th ed. (DSM-5), 2013. [doi:10.1176/appi.books.9780890425596](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.books.9780890425596).
- **The idea:** a panic attack is a sudden surge of intense fear or discomfort that peaks within minutes, diagnosed by a set number of symptoms from a list of 13. Most are body sensations, felt from inside: for example a pounding heart, trembling, a choking feeling, feeling lightheaded, and chills or heat.
- **Anxiety:** feeling restless, keyed up, or on edge is one of the criteria for generalized anxiety disorder.
- **Depression:** the first criterion describes a depressed mood with being sad, hopeless, or **empty** as examples, so emptiness is a recognized way low mood is felt, not only a figure of speech. Others include fatigue or loss of energy, trouble thinking or concentrating, and sleeping too little or too much.
- Taken from secondary sources that reproduce the criteria, including an [Open University course page](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/health-sports-psychology/panic-attacks-what-they-are-and-what-do-about-them/content-section-1.1), read 2026-11-16; the manual itself wasn't read.

## Usage terms

A copyrighted clinical manual. We use it to check that a sensation is a recognized one; we don't reproduce its criteria.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Fear's sensations are well documented.** A racing heart, shaky hands, a tight throat, short breath, lightheadedness, and chills are all recognized body signs of fear, not just figures of speech.
2. **Numbness in a panic attack is physical** (numbness or tingling, often in the hands), not the emotional numbness of Numb / empty, which is backed by depression's "empty" instead.
3. **Depression's symptoms back the low-energy sensations:** Drained, Foggy head, and Want to sleep.
4. **The same list covers many feelings.** These are signs of strong arousal, which is why the body wheel offers more than fear for several of them.
