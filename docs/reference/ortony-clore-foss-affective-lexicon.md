# Ortony, Clore and Foss: feelings and what happened

Why the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md) tags words for what happened to you (Betrayed, Abandoned, Safe) and puts them on the outer ring.

## What it is

- Andrew Ortony, Gerald L. Clore and Mark A. Foss, "The referential structure of the affective lexicon", *Cognitive Science* 11(3), 341–364, 1987. [doi:10.1207/s15516709cog1103_4](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1103_4).
- Gerald L. Clore, Andrew Ortony and Mark A. Foss, "The psychological foundations of the affective lexicon", *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 53(4), 751–766, 1987. [doi:10.1037/0022-3514.53.4.751](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.53.4.751).
- **The idea:** "Being angry is an emotion, but being abandoned is not". "Feeling abandoned" reports what happened, which might bring hurt, fear, anger, or sadness. They list such words (abandoned, cheated, insulted, neglected, safe) as objective descriptions of what happened. Lonely and hurt count as emotions.
- **The test:** 585 words rated in "feeling ___" and "being ___" frames. Emotion words scored high in both; words for what happened scored as emotions only after "feeling".
- They also class tired, exhausted, and sleepy as body states, and bored and confused as states of mind.
- Both papers were read in full, from scans, during the opinionated wheel's research (2026-11-19). Betrayed and Rejected aren't on their list; treating them the same way is our inference.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Words for what happened to you stay,** since people reach for them, but as a tagged list on the outer ring, and their cards still read as something felt.
2. **Body states belong on the body wheel,** not the feelings wheel.
