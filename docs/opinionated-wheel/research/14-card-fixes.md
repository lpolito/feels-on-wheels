# Fixing the flagged cards

On 2026-11-25 Lucas asked for the cards his review flagged ([research/13](13-definitions-review.md)), plus the 15 that research/13's cold test found really off, to be rewritten by the revised rules ([rules.md](../rules.md), Definitions), then cold-checked. The main session wrote 48 cards; a cold checker reviewed them (29 keep, 19 revise, 0 revert; its report follows); the main session took its revisions, with its own wording for Tenderness, Heartache, and Nervous, and reworded Worried so it no longer shares "over and over" with Fed up. Cards research/13 already settled the same day (Rejected, Left out, Jealous, Cringe, Empty) aren't repeated here.

Left as they were, by the rules: Trapped (the parent is allowed), Irritable (a right-now mood), Moved (what a feeling makes you do is allowed), Mortified, and Sorry for someone's label.

## The cold check of the first draft

It checked the first draft, so some wordings it quotes were replaced by its own suggestions above.

Totals: **29 keep, 19 revise, 0 revert.** None is worse than the card it replaced, so none needs a revert. Two come close (Defeated and Abandoned), and the revisions below fix them.

### Children that no longer fit

- **Heartache → Sorry for someone:** held only by the cover clause. Without it, this is a placement question.
- **Nervous → Uneasy:** loose, since Uneasy isn't about what's coming. It's a placement note, not a card fix.
- **Terrified → Horrified:** fits only if the card keeps fear (hence the revision).
- **Affection → Cared for:** being cared for (receiving) vs. affection (giving). This predates the rewrite, but the rewrite doesn't help.

### Template check

There's no shared template. In the 48 new cards:

- No first word appears more than twice.
- Only 7 use ", and …", and only 4 end in a wanting, wishing, urge, or tempted clause.
- No two-word ending repeats.

Three smaller drifts are worth watching:

1. **"Someone or something" as the stock fix for "too limiting."** Five new cards use it (Disillusioned, Heartache, Suspicious, Contempt, Hatred), for 10 on the wheel. Three of them sit near each other on Lucas's "reads alike" axis.
2. **"Feeling that you…".** Eight wheel cards open this way (Valued, Ashamed, Worthless, Inadequate, Inferior, Overwhelmed, Out of place, Guilty). Two of them are new, and the worth cluster in Sad reads as one voice.
3. **"Deep" and "Deeply".** Six cards open this way (Fulfilled, Grief, Heartbroken, Miserable, Hatred, Remorse). Three of those are new or rewritten, and the revisions above drop two of them.

"Over and over" also appears in both Worried and Fed up.
