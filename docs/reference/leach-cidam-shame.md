# Leach and Cidam: shame doesn't always mean hiding

Why Ashamed's card on the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md) doesn't say shame makes you hide.

## What it is

- Colin Wayne Leach and Atilla Cidam, "When is shame linked to constructive approach orientation? A meta-analysis", *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 109(6), 983–1002, 2015. [doi:10.1037/pspa0000037](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspa0000037).
- **What they found:** across 90 samples, shame went with trying to fix things when the failure could be fixed, and with withdrawing when it couldn't.
- Taken from the abstract, checked twice during the opinionated wheel's research.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **A card says only what's true for most people who feel it,** and hiding isn't.
