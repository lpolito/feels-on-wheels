# v1 content review

One entry per word. A word in two places on the wheel is listed once, where it first appears. Edit freely: definitions live in `src/data/locales/en.json`, and each feeling's sensations in `src/data/feelings.json`. The italic lines are what a feeling's card shows under "You might notice" (`en.json` → `sensationPhrases`); the wheel shows shorter labels (`en.json` → `sensations`).

## Fearful

- **Fearful**: Sensing danger or that something bad might happen, and wanting to protect yourself.  
  _A racing heart · Short, shallow breaths · Tense shoulders · Wanting to run_
  - **Scared**: A sudden feeling that you are in danger or about to be hurt.  
    _A racing heart · Your heart in your throat · Feeling frozen · Wanting to run_
    - **Helpless**: Feeling that you can't do anything to change what is happening to you.  
      _Heaviness · Feeling frozen · Wanting to give up_
    - **Frightened**: Strongly afraid, often because of something sudden or threatening.  
      _A tight throat · Chills · A racing heart · Shaky hands_
  - **Anxious**: Worried and uneasy about something that might happen, often without knowing exactly what.  
    _A knot in your stomach · A tight chest · Feeling restless · Wanting to control things_
    - **Overwhelmed**: Feeling like there is too much to handle at once, more than you have room for.  
      _A tight chest · A headache · A weight on your shoulders · Wanting to numb out_
    - **Worried**: Thinking again and again about something that could go wrong.  
      _A sinking feeling · Wanting to fix things · A knot in your stomach · Tense shoulders_
  - **Insecure**: Unsure of yourself or your place, and doubting that you are good enough.  
    _A knot in your stomach · Feeling restless · Wanting to hide · Wanting to be close_
    - **Inadequate**: Feeling that you are not good enough or not able to do what is needed.  
      _Heaviness · Wanting to hide · Wanting to give up_
    - **Inferior**: Feeling lower or less worthy than other people.  
      _Wanting to hide · A hot face_
  - **Weak**: Feeling that you lack the strength, power, or will to cope.  
    _Heaviness · Weak knees · Feeling drained · Shaky hands_
    - **Worthless**: Feeling that you have no value or that you don't matter.  
      _A hollow feeling in your chest · Heaviness · Wanting to hide_
    - **Insignificant**: Feeling small and unimportant, as if no one would notice you.  
      _Wanting to hide · A hollow feeling in your chest_
  - **Rejected**: Feeling pushed away or not wanted by someone.  
    _A hollow feeling in your chest · A lump in your throat · A sinking feeling · Wanting to hide_
    - **Excluded**: Feeling left out, as if you are on the outside of a group.  
      _A hollow feeling in your chest · A lump in your throat · Wanting to be close_
    - **Persecuted**: Feeling that you are being targeted or treated badly on purpose.  
      _A racing heart · Tense shoulders · Clenched teeth · Wanting to run_
  - **Threatened**: Sensing that someone or something might harm you or what you care about.  
    _Wanting to run · A racing heart · Tense shoulders · Your heart in your throat_
    - **Nervous**: Jittery and on edge about something coming up.  
      _Butterflies in your stomach · Sweaty palms · A dry mouth · Your heart in your throat_
    - **Exposed**: Feeling seen in a way you didn't choose, with nowhere to hide.  
      _Wanting to hide · A hot face_

## Angry

- **Angry**: A strong feeling that something is wrong or unfair, with an urge to push back.  
  _A hot face · Clenched teeth · Tight fists · A racing heart_
  - **Let Down**: Disappointed because someone didn't do what you hoped or expected.  
    _A sinking feeling · A heavy heart · A hollow feeling in your chest_
    - **Betrayed**: Hurt and angry because someone you trusted broke that trust.  
      _A knot in your stomach · Wanting to lash out · A hollow feeling in your chest · A lump in your throat_
    - **Resentful**: Holding on to anger about being treated unfairly in the past.  
      _Clenched teeth · A tight chest · Wanting to lash out · Tense shoulders_
  - **Humiliated**: Deeply embarrassed and made to feel small, often in front of others.  
    _A hot face · Wanting to hide · A sinking feeling_
    - **Disrespected**: Feeling that someone treated you as if you didn't matter.  
      _A hot face · Clenched teeth · A tight chest_
    - **Ridiculed**: Feeling mocked or laughed at.  
      _A hot face · Wanting to hide_
  - **Bitter**: Lasting anger and hurt about something that felt unfair.  
    _Wanting to lash out · Clenched teeth · A tight chest · A knot in your stomach_
    - **Indignant**: Angry about something you see as unjust or insulting.  
      _A hot face · Tense shoulders · Clenched teeth_
    - **Violated**: Feeling that a boundary was crossed or something of yours was taken without permission.  
      _Your skin crawling · A knot in your stomach · Feeling queasy · Feeling frozen_
  - **Mad**: Plainly angry and fired up.  
    _Clenched teeth · Tight fists · A racing heart · A hot face_
    - **Furious**: Extremely angry, close to losing control.  
      _Shaky hands · Wanting to lash out · A hot face · Tight fists_
    - **Jealous**: Afraid of losing someone or something to another person, or wanting what they have.  
      _Wanting to control things · A knot in your stomach · A tight chest · A hot face_
  - **Aggressive**: Ready to attack or confront, in words or actions.  
    _Tight fists · Tense shoulders · Wanting to lash out_
    - **Provoked**: Stirred up to anger by something someone said or did.  
      _Wanting to lash out · A hot face · Clenched teeth · A racing heart_
    - **Hostile**: Unfriendly and ready to fight or oppose.  
      _Wanting to lash out · Clenched teeth · Tense shoulders · Tight fists_
  - **Frustrated**: Upset because something keeps getting in your way.  
    _A headache · Tense shoulders · Tight fists · Wanting to cry_
    - **Infuriated**: Made extremely angry, usually by something that keeps happening.  
      _A hot face · Tight fists · A racing heart_
    - **Annoyed**: Mildly irritated by something that bothers you.  
      _Tense shoulders · Feeling restless · Clenched teeth_
  - **Distant**: Pulled back from others, keeping feelings at arm's length.  
    _Feeling numb or empty · Feeling drained · Wanting to be alone_
    - **Withdrawn**: Pulling into yourself and avoiding contact with others.  
      _Wanting to be alone · Wanting to hide · Feeling drained_
    - **Numb**: Feeling very little, as if your emotions have been switched off.  
      _Not feeling much · Feeling numb or empty · Heaviness · A foggy head_
  - **Critical**: Quick to notice faults and judge them.  
    _Clenched teeth · Tense shoulders_
    - **Skeptical**: Doubting whether something is true or can be trusted.  
      _Tense shoulders · Wanting to back away_
    - **Dismissive**: Brushing something off as not worth your attention.  
      _Wanting to back away · Not feeling much_

## Disgusted

- **Disgusted**: Strong dislike of something that feels gross, wrong, or offensive.  
  _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away_
  - **Disapproving**: Feeling that something is wrong or not okay.  
    _Clenched teeth · Wanting to back away · Tense shoulders_
    - **Judgmental**: Forming harsh opinions about someone or something.  
      _Clenched teeth · Tense shoulders · Wanting to back away_
    - **Embarrassed**: Self-conscious and uncomfortable after something awkward or revealing.  
      _A hot face · Wanting to hide_
  - **Disappointed**: Sad that something didn't turn out the way you hoped.  
    _A sinking feeling · A heavy heart · A hollow feeling in your chest_
    - **Appalled**: Shocked and disgusted by something you think is very wrong.  
      _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away · Feeling frozen_
    - **Revolted**: Strongly repelled, with an urge to get away.  
      _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away_
  - **Awful**: Feeling very bad, sick, or unpleasant inside.  
    _Feeling queasy · A knot in your stomach · Heaviness_
    - **Nauseated**: Sick to your stomach, as if you might throw up.  
      _Feeling queasy · Cold hands_
    - **Detestable**: Feeling that something, or you yourself, is hateful or deeply unpleasant.  
      _Feeling queasy · Wanting to back away · A knot in your stomach_
  - **Repelled**: Pushed away by something you find unpleasant.  
    _Feeling queasy · Your skin crawling · Wanting to back away_
    - **Horrified**: Filled with shock and fear at something terrible.  
      _Your skin crawling · Wanting to back away · Your heart in your throat · Feeling frozen_
    - **Hesitant**: Holding back because you are unsure or uneasy.  
      _Wanting to back away · Feeling frozen · Feeling restless · Short, shallow breaths_

## Sad

- **Sad**: A heavy feeling of loss, disappointment, or hurt.  
  _Heaviness · A lump in your throat · A heavy heart · Wanting to cry_
  - **Hurt**: Emotional pain from something said, done, or lost.  
    _A lump in your throat · A hollow feeling in your chest · Wanting to cry · Wanting to lash out_
    - **Embarrassed**: see above
    - **Disappointed**: see above
  - **Depressed**: Low and heavy for a long time, with little energy or hope.  
    _A foggy head · A heavy heart · Heaviness · Wanting to sleep_
    - **Inferior**: see above
    - **Empty**: Feeling hollow, as if something important is missing.  
      _A hollow feeling in your chest · Feeling numb or empty · Feeling drained_
  - **Guilty**: Feeling that you did something wrong and wanting to make it right.  
    _A lump in your throat · A heavy heart · A knot in your stomach · Wanting to fix things_
    - **Remorseful**: Deeply sorry for something you did.  
      _A lump in your throat · A heavy heart · Wanting to fix things_
    - **Ashamed**: Feeling that something is wrong with who you are, not just with what you did.  
      _A hot face · Wanting to hide · Wanting to numb out_
  - **Despair**: Losing all hope that things can get better.  
    _Heaviness · Wanting to give up · Wanting to cry_
    - **Powerless**: Feeling unable to affect what happens to you.  
      _A weight on your shoulders · Feeling frozen · Wanting to give up · Heaviness_
    - **Grief**: Deep sorrow after losing someone or something you love.  
      _A lump in your throat · Wanting to cry · A hollow feeling in your chest · Feeling drained_
  - **Vulnerable**: Open to being hurt, without your usual protection.  
    _Wanting to hide · A tight chest · Shaky hands_
    - **Fragile**: Feeling easily broken, as if you can't take much more.  
      _Wanting to be alone · Shaky hands · Wanting to cry · Short, shallow breaths_
    - **Victimized**: Feeling that you were harmed or treated unfairly by others.  
      _A tight chest · Wanting to hide · A knot in your stomach_
  - **Lonely**: Wanting connection and feeling alone without it.  
    _A hollow feeling in your chest · Wanting to be close · Wanting to numb out · Heaviness_
    - **Abandoned**: Feeling left behind by someone you counted on.  
      _A hollow feeling in your chest · A lump in your throat · Wanting to cry_
    - **Isolated**: Cut off from other people.  
      _A hollow feeling in your chest · Feeling drained · Wanting to be close_

## Happy

- **Happy**: A light, good feeling of pleasure or well-being.  
  _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Feeling light · Feeling relaxed_
  - **Optimistic**: Expecting that things will turn out well.  
    _Feeling tall · Feeling light · Feeling relaxed_
    - **Inspired**: Moved to create, act, or do something meaningful.  
      _A buzzing feeling · Chills · Feeling tall_
    - **Hopeful**: Believing that something good can happen.  
      _Feeling light · Warmth or fullness in your chest · Feeling relaxed_
  - **Trusting**: Feeling safe relying on someone or something.  
    _Feeling relaxed · Warmth or fullness in your chest_
    - **Intimate**: Feeling close and deeply connected to someone.  
      _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Feeling relaxed · Wanting to be close_
    - **Sensitive**: Easily moved, and keenly aware of your own feelings and other people's.  
      _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Wanting to cry · A hot face_
  - **Peaceful**: Calm and settled, without inner conflict.  
    _Not feeling much · Feeling relaxed · Feeling light_
    - **Thankful**: Glad and appreciative for something you received.  
      _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Wanting to cry · Feeling relaxed_
    - **Loving**: Feeling deep care and affection for someone.  
      _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Wanting to fix things · Wanting to be close · Feeling relaxed_
  - **Powerful**: Feeling strong and able to make things happen.  
    _Feeling tall · Feeling relaxed · A buzzing feeling_
    - **Creative**: Full of ideas and wanting to make something new.  
      _A buzzing feeling · Feeling light · Feeling restless_
    - **Courageous**: Facing fear or difficulty and acting anyway.  
      _Feeling tall · A racing heart_
  - **Accepted**: Feeling welcomed and included as you are.  
    _Feeling relaxed · Warmth or fullness in your chest_
    - **Valued**: Feeling that others see your worth and appreciate you.  
      _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Feeling tall_
    - **Respected**: Feeling that others take you seriously and treat you well.

  - **Proud**: Pleased with something you or someone close to you did.  
    _Warmth or fullness in your chest · Feeling tall_
    - **Confident**: Sure of yourself and your abilities.  
      _Feeling tall · Feeling relaxed_
    - **Successful**: Feeling that you reached a goal or did well.  
      _Feeling tall · Warmth or fullness in your chest · A buzzing feeling_
  - **Interested**: Wanting to know more about something.  
    _A buzzing feeling · Feeling restless_
    - **Inquisitive**: Eager to ask questions and find things out.  
      _Feeling restless · A buzzing feeling_
    - **Curious**: Drawn to explore something new or unknown.  
      _A buzzing feeling · Feeling restless_
  - **Content**: Satisfied with how things are right now.  
    _Not feeling much · Feeling relaxed · Feeling light_
    - **Joyful**: Full of great happiness and delight.  
      _Feeling light · A buzzing feeling · Warmth or fullness in your chest_
    - **Free**: Unburdened and able to be yourself.  
      _Feeling light · Feeling relaxed · Feeling tall_
  - **Playful**: In the mood for fun and lightheartedness.  
    _Feeling light · A buzzing feeling_
    - **Cheeky**: Playfully bold or a little mischievous.  
      _A buzzing feeling · Feeling light_
    - **Aroused**: Feeling physical or sexual excitement.  
      _Weak knees · Warmth or fullness in your chest · A racing heart · A hot face_

## Surprised

- **Surprised**: A quick reaction to something unexpected.  
  _Your heart in your throat · A racing heart_
  - **Excited**: Eager and full of energy about something.  
    _Butterflies in your stomach · A racing heart · A buzzing feeling · Shaky hands_
    - **Energetic**: Full of energy and ready to go.  
      _A buzzing feeling · Feeling restless · Feeling tall_
    - **Eager**: Keen and impatient for something to happen.  
      _Feeling restless · A buzzing feeling · Butterflies in your stomach_
  - **Amazed**: Very surprised and impressed.

    - **Awe**: Wonder at something vast, beautiful, or powerful.  
      _Chills · Your heart in your throat · Feeling relaxed · Feeling light_
    - **Astonished**: Greatly surprised, almost unable to believe it.

  - **Confused**: Unable to make sense of something.  
    _A foggy head · Feeling lightheaded_
    - **Perplexed**: Puzzled by something that doesn't add up.

    - **Disillusioned**: Let down after learning something isn't as good as you believed.

  - **Startled**: Briefly jolted by something sudden.  
    _Your heart in your throat · A racing heart_
    - **Dismayed**: Upset and discouraged by something unexpected.

    - **Shocked**: Stunned by something sudden and upsetting.  
      _Feeling lightheaded · Feeling frozen · Feeling numb or empty · Your heart in your throat_

## Bad

- **Bad**: Generally unwell or off, without one clear emotion.  
  _Feeling drained · Heaviness · A foggy head_
  - **Tired**: Low on energy and needing rest.  
    _A foggy head · Feeling drained · Wanting to sleep · Heaviness_
    - **Unfocused**: Having trouble keeping your attention on one thing.  
      _A foggy head · Feeling restless · Feeling drained_
    - **Sleepy**: Ready for sleep, with heavy eyes and a slow body.  
      _Feeling drained · Heaviness · Wanting to sleep_
  - **Stressed**: Under pressure and stretched thin.  
    _A headache · Clenched teeth · Tense shoulders · Short, shallow breaths_
    - **Out of Control**: Feeling that things are happening faster than you can manage.  
      _Wanting to control things · A racing heart · Short, shallow breaths · Feeling restless_
    - **Overwhelmed**: see above
  - **Busy**: Occupied with a lot to do.  
    _Feeling restless · Tense shoulders · Short, shallow breaths_
    - **Rushed**: Hurrying, without enough time to do things well.  
      _A racing heart · Short, shallow breaths · Feeling restless_
    - **Pressured**: Pushed by demands or expectations from others or yourself.  
      _A weight on your shoulders · A tight chest · Tense shoulders · A headache_
  - **Bored**: Uninterested and wanting something to happen.  
    _Feeling restless · Feeling drained · Wanting to sleep_
    - **Apathetic**: Not caring or feeling much about anything.  
      _Feeling drained · Feeling numb or empty · Not feeling much_
    - **Indifferent**: Neither for nor against something, without strong feelings either way.  
      _Feeling numb or empty · Not feeling much · Feeling drained_


## Body wheel

The body sensations wheel after the [body wheel rework](superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-design.md) (with its [changes](superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-changes.md)): body areas, the sensations under each, and the 2–3 feelings each sensation leads to. These are drafted to agree with each feeling's "You might notice" above, though nothing enforces it. The wheel shows the short labels below; cards show the phrases above.

### Head / face

- **Headache**: Stressed, Frustrated
- **Foggy head**: Confused, Tired, Depressed
- **Lightheaded**: Shocked, Overwhelmed, Nervous
- **Hot face**: Embarrassed, Ashamed, Humiliated

### Throat / mouth

- **Lump in throat**: Hurt, Grief, Guilty
- **Heart in throat**: Scared, Nervous, Startled
- **Tight throat**: Anxious, Frightened, Repelled
- **Dry mouth**: Nervous, Scared
- **Clenched teeth**: Mad, Resentful, Stressed

### Shoulders

- **Tense shoulders**: Stressed, Anxious, Frustrated
- **Weight on shoulders**: Pressured, Overwhelmed, Powerless

### Hands

- **Tight fists**: Mad, Frustrated
- **Shaky hands**: Scared, Furious, Excited
- **Sweaty palms**: Nervous, Excited
- **Cold hands**: Scared, Nervous

### Chest

- **Racing heart**: Scared, Mad, Excited
- **Tight chest**: Anxious, Resentful
- **Short breath**: Anxious, Stressed
- **Hollow chest**: Lonely, Rejected, Empty
- **Heavy heart**: Guilty, Let Down, Depressed

### Stomach

- **Knot in stomach**: Anxious, Guilty, Betrayed
- **Sinking feeling**: Disappointed, Dismayed, Worried
- **Butterflies**: Nervous, Excited
- **Queasy**: Repelled, Anxious

### Whole body

- **Restless**: Anxious, Bored, Eager
- **Buzzing**: Excited, Inspired, Anxious
- **Chills**: Frightened, Awe, Inspired
- **Frozen**: Scared, Shocked, Powerless
- **Skin crawling**: Repelled, Violated
- **Heaviness**: Depressed, Weak, Helpless
- **Drained**: Tired, Apathetic
- **Numb / empty**: Numb, Empty, Shocked
- **Nothing much**: Content, Peaceful, Apathetic
- **Weak knees**: Scared, Weak, Aroused

### Urges (on the wheel: "The urge to…")

- **Run**: Scared, Threatened, Overwhelmed
- **Hide**: Ashamed, Exposed, Vulnerable
- **Lash out**: Furious, Provoked, Hurt
- **Back away**: Revolted, Disapproving
- **Fix things**: Guilty, Worried, Loving
- **Be close**: Loving, Lonely, Insecure
- **Be alone**: Withdrawn, Fragile, Overwhelmed
- **Give up**: Despair, Inadequate, Helpless
- **Numb out**: Overwhelmed, Ashamed, Lonely
- **Sleep**: Tired, Bored, Depressed
- **Cry**: Frustrated, Overwhelmed, Thankful
- **Control things**: Out of Control, Anxious, Jealous

On cards only, not on the wheel: Warmth or fullness in your chest, Feeling light, Feeling relaxed, Feeling tall.
