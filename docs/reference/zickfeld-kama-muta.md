# Zickfeld and others: being moved

Why Moved is on the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md) as a word, not a family.

## What it is

- Janis H. Zickfeld and others, "Kama muta: Conceptualizing and measuring the experience often labelled being moved across 19 nations and 15 languages", *Emotion* 19(3), 402–424, 2019. [doi:10.1037/emo0000450](https://doi.org/10.1037/emo0000450).
- **What they found:** 3,542 people in 19 countries described being moved or touched in consistent ways: warmth, tears, chills, and a sense of sudden closeness.
- **Limits, by the authors:** mostly European students, self-report only, and nearly all the evidence on it comes from one group.
- The paper was read during the opinionated wheel's research.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Moved is a real feeling,** under Love → Compassion, narrowed to being moved by someone's suffering or courage.
