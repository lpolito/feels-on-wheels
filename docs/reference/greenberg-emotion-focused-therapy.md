# Leslie Greenberg: emotion-focused therapy

The research behind **Hurt** under the body wheel's **Lash out**, a change made after the [body wheel rework spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-design.md) was approved (see its [changes](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-changes.md)).

## What it is

- Leslie S. Greenberg, a psychologist at York University in Toronto, developed emotion-focused therapy (EFT). *Emotion-Focused Therapy: Coaching Clients to Work Through Their Feelings*, American Psychological Association, 2002; a second edition followed ([publisher](https://www.apa.org/pubs/books/emotion-focused-therapy-second-edition)).
- Also: Robert Elliott and Leslie S. Greenberg, "The essence of process-experiential/emotion-focused therapy", *American Journal of Psychotherapy* 61(3), 2007. A postprint is at [strathprints.strath.ac.uk/27735](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/27735/).
- **The idea:** EFT tells apart a **primary** emotion, the first direct response to a situation, and a **secondary** one, a reaction to that first feeling. Anger is often secondary, covering hurt, sadness, or fear, though anger at being wronged can also be primary and healthy.
- Taken from the 2007 paper's postprint as summarized in search results, and from secondary summaries, read 2026-11-16; neither book was read.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use the idea, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **The pull to lash out can come from hurt.** Offering Hurt next to Furious and Provoked under Lash out gives a path to what's often under anger, without claiming it's what's under anyone's anger in particular.
2. **Anger isn't always covering something,** so the angry feelings stay on the same urge.
