# Zupan, Dempsey and Hartwell: which category people pick for a word

Evidence for which core people would tap, in the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md).

## What it is

- Barbra Zupan, Lynn Dempsey and Katelyn Hartwell, "Categorising emotion words: The influence of response options", *Language and Cognition* 15(1), 29–52, 2023 (online 2022). [doi:10.1017/langcog.2022.24](https://doi.org/10.1017/langcog.2022.24); [Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language-and-cognition/article/categorising-emotion-words-the-influence-of-response-options/88E3D824F5880D022FED2BB5CEAAA860); data on [OSF](https://osf.io/uw95c).
- **What they did:** 89 raters scored how well each of 497 feeling words fits Happy, Sad, Angry, Fearful, and Neutral; in a second study, 56 raters scored 24 of the words against 13 categories.
- **What we used:** from their raw data, the share of raters whose top score went to each category, the closest thing to a first tap.
- **Their own finding:** adding categories moved words (guilty and overwhelmed moved from Sad to Anxiety), so which cores a wheel offers changes where borderline words go.
- The paper and its open data were read during the opinionated wheel's research (2026-11-22). The raters were mostly women at one Canadian university, and the options had no Love and no catch-all.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Words in two places come from splits here:** Betrayed (54% Angry, 32% Sad) and Empty (69% Sad, though it's also Numb's hollow feeling).
2. **Uneasy and Insecure under Scared,** and Insulted under Angry, by where raters put them.
