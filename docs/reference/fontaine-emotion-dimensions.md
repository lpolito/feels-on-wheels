# Fontaine and others: what separates anger from fear

Why the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md) has no rule about matching a word's energy to its parent's.

## What it is

- Johnny R. J. Fontaine, Klaus R. Scherer, Etienne B. Roesch and Phoebe C. Ellsworth, "The world of emotions is not two-dimensional", *Psychological Science* 18(12), 1050–1057, 2007. [doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.02024.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2007.02024.x).
- **What they found:** four dimensions, in order: pleasantness, power (or control), energy, and novelty. What separates anger from fear is a sense of power, not energy.
- The four dimensions and their order are in the abstract (which calls novelty "unpredictability"). That power, not energy, separates anger from fear comes from the research reports' reading of the paper and the related GRID work, not the abstract; the full text wasn't read.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use its findings, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Energy doesn't organize the families:** irritation and rage both belong to anger.
