# v1 content review

One entry per word. A word in two places on the wheel is listed once, where it first appears. Edit freely: definitions live in `src/data/locales/en.json`, sensations in `src/data/feelings.json` (labels in `en.json` → `sensations`).

## Fearful

- **Fearful**: Sensing danger or that something bad might happen, and wanting to protect yourself.  
  _Racing heart · Shallow breathing · Tense shoulders_
  - **Scared**: A sudden feeling that you are in danger or about to be hurt.  
    _Racing heart · Frozen or very still · Wide eyes_
    - **Helpless**: Feeling that you can't do anything to change what is happening to you.  
      _Heavy arms and legs · Frozen or very still · Shallow breathing_
    - **Frightened**: Strongly afraid, often because of something sudden or threatening.  
      _Racing heart · Trembling or shaky · Sharp intake of breath_
  - **Anxious**: Worried and uneasy about something that might happen, often without knowing exactly what.  
    _Knot in your stomach · Restless, need to move · Shallow breathing_
    - **Overwhelmed**: Feeling like there is too much to handle at once, more than you have room for.  
      _Tight chest · Foggy head · Shallow breathing · Headache_
    - **Worried**: Thinking again and again about something that could go wrong.  
      _Knot in your stomach · Tense shoulders · Fidgety_
  - **Insecure**: Unsure of yourself or your place, and doubting that you are good enough.  
    _Urge to shrink or hide · Knot in your stomach · Fidgety_
    - **Inadequate**: Feeling that you are not good enough or not able to do what is needed.  
      _Urge to shrink or hide · Slumped posture · Heavy arms and legs_
    - **Inferior**: Feeling lower or less worthy than other people.  
      _Urge to shrink or hide · Slumped posture · Hot or flushed face_
  - **Weak**: Feeling that you lack the strength, power, or will to cope.  
    _Heavy arms and legs · Low energy · Trembling or shaky_
    - **Worthless**: Feeling that you have no value or that you don't matter.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Slumped posture · Heavy arms and legs_
    - **Insignificant**: Feeling small and unimportant, as if no one would notice you.  
      _Urge to shrink or hide · Hollow feeling in your chest · Slumped posture_
  - **Rejected**: Feeling pushed away or not wanted by someone.  
    _Hollow feeling in your chest · Lump in your throat · Urge to turn away_
    - **Excluded**: Feeling left out, as if you are on the outside of a group.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Lump in your throat · Urge to shrink or hide_
    - **Persecuted**: Feeling that you are being targeted or treated badly on purpose.  
      _Tense shoulders · Racing heart · Clenched jaw_
  - **Threatened**: Sensing that someone or something might harm you or what you care about.  
    _Racing heart · Tense shoulders · Wide eyes_
    - **Nervous**: Jittery and on edge about something coming up.  
      _Butterflies in your stomach · Sweaty palms · Fidgety_
    - **Exposed**: Feeling seen in a way you didn't choose, with nowhere to hide.  
      _Blushing · Hot or flushed face · Urge to shrink or hide_

## Angry

- **Angry**: A strong feeling that something is wrong or unfair, with an urge to push back.  
  _Hot or flushed face · Clenched jaw · Clenched fists · Racing heart_
  - **Let Down**: Disappointed because someone didn't do what you hoped or expected.  
    _Heavy arms and legs · Hollow feeling in your chest · Slumped posture_
    - **Betrayed**: Hurt and angry because someone you trusted broke that trust.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Knot in your stomach · Lump in your throat_
    - **Resentful**: Holding on to anger about being treated unfairly in the past.  
      _Clenched jaw · Tense shoulders · Tight chest_
  - **Humiliated**: Deeply embarrassed and made to feel small, often in front of others.  
    _Hot or flushed face · Blushing · Urge to shrink or hide_
    - **Disrespected**: Feeling that someone treated you as if you didn't matter.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Clenched jaw · Tight chest_
    - **Ridiculed**: Feeling mocked or laughed at.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Blushing · Urge to turn away_
  - **Bitter**: Lasting anger and hurt about something that felt unfair.  
    _Clenched jaw · Tight chest · Knot in your stomach_
    - **Indignant**: Angry about something you see as unjust or insulting.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Tense shoulders · Clenched jaw_
    - **Violated**: Feeling that a boundary was crossed or something of yours was taken without permission.  
      _Knot in your stomach · Queasy stomach · Frozen or very still_
  - **Mad**: Plainly angry and fired up.  
    _Hot or flushed face · Clenched fists · Racing heart_
    - **Furious**: Extremely angry, close to losing control.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Clenched fists · Racing heart · Trembling or shaky_
    - **Jealous**: Afraid of losing someone or something to another person, or wanting what they have.  
      _Knot in your stomach · Tight chest · Hot or flushed face_
  - **Aggressive**: Ready to attack or confront, in words or actions.  
    _Clenched fists · Tense shoulders · Restless, need to move_
    - **Provoked**: Stirred up to anger by something someone said or did.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Clenched jaw · Racing heart_
    - **Hostile**: Unfriendly and ready to fight or oppose.  
      _Clenched jaw · Tense shoulders · Clenched fists_
  - **Frustrated**: Upset because something keeps getting in your way.  
    _Tense shoulders · Clenched jaw · Restless, need to move_
    - **Infuriated**: Made extremely angry, usually by something that keeps happening.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Clenched fists · Racing heart_
    - **Annoyed**: Mildly irritated by something that bothers you.  
      _Tense shoulders · Fidgety · Scrunched-up face_
  - **Distant**: Pulled back from others, keeping feelings at arm's length.  
    _Numbness · Urge to turn away · Low energy_
    - **Withdrawn**: Pulling into yourself and avoiding contact with others.  
      _Urge to shrink or hide · Urge to turn away · Low energy_
    - **Numb**: Feeling very little, as if your emotions have been switched off.  
      _Numbness · Heavy arms and legs · Foggy head_
  - **Critical**: Quick to notice faults and judge them.  
    _Scrunched-up face · Tense shoulders · Clenched jaw_
    - **Skeptical**: Doubting whether something is true or can be trusted.  
      _Raised eyebrows · Scrunched-up face · Tense shoulders_
    - **Dismissive**: Brushing something off as not worth your attention.  
      _Urge to turn away · Raised eyebrows_

## Disgusted

- **Disgusted**: Strong dislike of something that feels gross, wrong, or offensive.  
  _Queasy stomach · Scrunched-up face · Urge to turn away_
  - **Disapproving**: Feeling that something is wrong or not okay.  
    _Scrunched-up face · Tense shoulders · Urge to turn away_
    - **Judgmental**: Forming harsh opinions about someone or something.  
      _Scrunched-up face · Raised eyebrows · Tense shoulders_
    - **Embarrassed**: Self-conscious and uncomfortable after something awkward or revealing.  
      _Blushing · Hot or flushed face · Urge to shrink or hide_
  - **Disappointed**: Sad that something didn't turn out the way you hoped.  
    _Heavy arms and legs · Slumped posture · Hollow feeling in your chest_
    - **Appalled**: Shocked and disgusted by something you think is very wrong.  
      _Sharp intake of breath · Wide eyes · Queasy stomach_
    - **Revolted**: Strongly repelled, with an urge to get away.  
      _Queasy stomach · Urge to turn away · Scrunched-up face_
  - **Awful**: Feeling very bad, sick, or unpleasant inside.  
    _Queasy stomach · Knot in your stomach · Heavy arms and legs_
    - **Nauseated**: Sick to your stomach, as if you might throw up.  
      _Queasy stomach · Cold hands · Sweaty palms_
    - **Detestable**: Feeling that something, or you yourself, is hateful or deeply unpleasant.  
      _Queasy stomach · Urge to turn away · Knot in your stomach_
  - **Repelled**: Pushed away by something you find unpleasant.  
    _Urge to turn away · Scrunched-up face · Queasy stomach_
    - **Horrified**: Filled with shock and fear at something terrible.  
      _Sharp intake of breath · Frozen or very still · Wide eyes · Cold hands_
    - **Hesitant**: Holding back because you are unsure or uneasy.  
      _Frozen or very still · Fidgety · Shallow breathing_

## Sad

- **Sad**: A heavy feeling of loss, disappointment, or hurt.  
  _Heavy arms and legs · Tearful · Lump in your throat · Slumped posture_
  - **Hurt**: Emotional pain from something said, done, or lost.  
    _Lump in your throat · Tearful · Hollow feeling in your chest_
    - **Embarrassed**: see above
    - **Disappointed**: see above
  - **Depressed**: Low and heavy for a long time, with little energy or hope.  
    _Heavy arms and legs · Low energy · Slumped posture · Foggy head_
    - **Inferior**: see above
    - **Empty**: Feeling hollow, as if something important is missing.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Numbness · Low energy_
  - **Guilty**: Feeling that you did something wrong and wanting to make it right.  
    _Knot in your stomach · Urge to shrink or hide · Tight chest_
    - **Remorseful**: Deeply sorry for something you did.  
      _Lump in your throat · Slumped posture · Tearful_
    - **Ashamed**: Feeling that something is wrong with who you are, not just with what you did.  
      _Hot or flushed face · Urge to shrink or hide · Urge to turn away_
  - **Despair**: Losing all hope that things can get better.  
    _Heavy arms and legs · Tearful · Slumped posture_
    - **Powerless**: Feeling unable to affect what happens to you.  
      _Heavy arms and legs · Frozen or very still · Low energy_
    - **Grief**: Deep sorrow after losing someone or something you love.  
      _Tearful · Lump in your throat · Hollow feeling in your chest · Tired eyes_
  - **Vulnerable**: Open to being hurt, without your usual protection.  
    _Urge to shrink or hide · Tight chest · Trembling or shaky_
    - **Fragile**: Feeling easily broken, as if you can't take much more.  
      _Trembling or shaky · Tearful · Shallow breathing_
    - **Victimized**: Feeling that you were harmed or treated unfairly by others.  
      _Tight chest · Urge to shrink or hide · Knot in your stomach_
  - **Lonely**: Wanting connection and feeling alone without it.  
    _Hollow feeling in your chest · Heavy arms and legs · Lump in your throat_
    - **Abandoned**: Feeling left behind by someone you counted on.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Lump in your throat · Tearful_
    - **Isolated**: Cut off from other people.  
      _Hollow feeling in your chest · Low energy · Urge to shrink or hide_

## Happy

- **Happy**: A light, good feeling of pleasure or well-being.  
  _Smiling · Light, floaty body · Warmth in your chest · Open, upright posture_
  - **Optimistic**: Expecting that things will turn out well.  
    _Open, upright posture · Light, floaty body · Slow, deep breathing_
    - **Inspired**: Moved to create, act, or do something meaningful.  
      _Buzzing energy · Open, upright posture · Wide eyes_
    - **Hopeful**: Believing that something good can happen.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Light, floaty body · Slow, deep breathing_
  - **Trusting**: Feeling safe relying on someone or something.  
    _Relaxed muscles · Slow, deep breathing · Open, upright posture_
    - **Intimate**: Feeling close and deeply connected to someone.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Relaxed muscles · Smiling_
    - **Sensitive**: Easily moved, and keenly aware of your own feelings and other people's.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Tearful · Blushing_
  - **Peaceful**: Calm and settled, without inner conflict.  
    _Relaxed muscles · Slow, deep breathing · Light, floaty body_
    - **Thankful**: Glad and appreciative for something you received.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Smiling · Slow, deep breathing_
    - **Loving**: Feeling deep care and affection for someone.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Smiling · Relaxed muscles_
  - **Powerful**: Feeling strong and able to make things happen.  
    _Open, upright posture · Slow, deep breathing · Buzzing energy_
    - **Creative**: Full of ideas and wanting to make something new.  
      _Buzzing energy · Light, floaty body · Restless, need to move_
    - **Courageous**: Facing fear or difficulty and acting anyway.  
      _Open, upright posture · Racing heart · Slow, deep breathing_
  - **Accepted**: Feeling welcomed and included as you are.  
    _Relaxed muscles · Warmth in your chest · Smiling_
    - **Valued**: Feeling that others see your worth and appreciate you.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Open, upright posture · Smiling_
    - **Respected**: Feeling that others take you seriously and treat you well.  
      _Open, upright posture · Slow, deep breathing · Relaxed muscles_
  - **Proud**: Pleased with something you or someone close to you did.  
    _Open, upright posture · Warmth in your chest · Smiling_
    - **Confident**: Sure of yourself and your abilities.  
      _Open, upright posture · Slow, deep breathing · Relaxed muscles_
    - **Successful**: Feeling that you reached a goal or did well.  
      _Open, upright posture · Smiling · Buzzing energy_
  - **Interested**: Wanting to know more about something.  
    _Wide eyes · Raised eyebrows · Open, upright posture_
    - **Inquisitive**: Eager to ask questions and find things out.  
      _Raised eyebrows · Wide eyes · Fidgety_
    - **Curious**: Drawn to explore something new or unknown.  
      _Wide eyes · Raised eyebrows · Buzzing energy_
  - **Content**: Satisfied with how things are right now.  
    _Relaxed muscles · Slow, deep breathing · Smiling_
    - **Joyful**: Full of great happiness and delight.  
      _Smiling · Light, floaty body · Buzzing energy · Warmth in your chest_
    - **Free**: Unburdened and able to be yourself.  
      _Light, floaty body · Slow, deep breathing · Open, upright posture_
  - **Playful**: In the mood for fun and lightheartedness.  
    _Smiling · Buzzing energy · Light, floaty body_
    - **Cheeky**: Playfully bold or a little mischievous.  
      _Smiling · Raised eyebrows · Buzzing energy_
    - **Aroused**: Feeling physical or sexual excitement.  
      _Warmth in your chest · Racing heart · Hot or flushed face_

## Surprised

- **Surprised**: A quick reaction to something unexpected.  
  _Wide eyes · Raised eyebrows · Sharp intake of breath_
  - **Excited**: Eager and full of energy about something.  
    _Buzzing energy · Racing heart · Butterflies in your stomach · Restless, need to move_
    - **Energetic**: Full of energy and ready to go.  
      _Buzzing energy · Restless, need to move · Open, upright posture_
    - **Eager**: Keen and impatient for something to happen.  
      _Buzzing energy · Butterflies in your stomach · Fidgety_
  - **Amazed**: Very surprised and impressed.  
    _Wide eyes · Sharp intake of breath · Raised eyebrows_
    - **Awe**: Wonder at something vast, beautiful, or powerful.  
      _Wide eyes · Slow, deep breathing · Light, floaty body_
    - **Astonished**: Greatly surprised, almost unable to believe it.  
      _Wide eyes · Sharp intake of breath · Frozen or very still_
  - **Confused**: Unable to make sense of something.  
    _Foggy head · Scrunched-up face · Raised eyebrows_
    - **Perplexed**: Puzzled by something that doesn't add up.  
      _Scrunched-up face · Foggy head · Raised eyebrows_
    - **Disillusioned**: Let down after learning something isn't as good as you believed.  
      _Heavy arms and legs · Slumped posture · Hollow feeling in your chest_
  - **Startled**: Briefly jolted by something sudden.  
    _Sharp intake of breath · Racing heart · Wide eyes_
    - **Dismayed**: Upset and discouraged by something unexpected.  
      _Sharp intake of breath · Knot in your stomach · Slumped posture_
    - **Shocked**: Stunned by something sudden and upsetting.

## Bad

- **Bad**: Generally unwell or off, without one clear emotion.  
  _Low energy · Heavy arms and legs · Foggy head_
  - **Tired**: Low on energy and needing rest.  
    _Tired eyes · Heavy arms and legs · Low energy_
    - **Unfocused**: Having trouble keeping your attention on one thing.  
      _Foggy head · Fidgety · Tired eyes_
    - **Sleepy**: Ready for sleep, with heavy eyes and a slow body.  
      _Tired eyes · Heavy arms and legs · Low energy_
  - **Stressed**: Under pressure and stretched thin.  
    _Tense shoulders · Headache · Tight chest · Shallow breathing_
    - **Out of Control**: Feeling that things are happening faster than you can manage.  
      _Racing heart · Shallow breathing · Restless, need to move_
    - **Overwhelmed**: see above
  - **Busy**: Occupied with a lot to do.  
    _Restless, need to move · Tense shoulders · Shallow breathing_
    - **Rushed**: Hurrying, without enough time to do things well.  
      _Racing heart · Shallow breathing · Restless, need to move_
    - **Pressured**: Pushed by demands or expectations from others or yourself.  
      _Tight chest · Tense shoulders · Headache_
  - **Bored**: Uninterested and wanting something to happen.  
    _Low energy · Fidgety · Slumped posture_
    - **Apathetic**: Not caring or feeling much about anything.  
      _Numbness · Low energy · Slumped posture_
    - **Indifferent**: Neither for nor against something, without strong feelings either way.  
      _Low energy · Numbness_


## Body wheel

The v2 body sensations wheel ([spec](superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-feelings-wheel-v2-body-wheel-design.md)): body areas, the sensations under each, and the 2–3 feelings each sensation leads to. These links are the body wheel's own; they don't have to match a feeling's "You might notice".

### Head and eyes

- **Headache**: Stressed, Frustrated, Overwhelmed
- **Foggy head**: Confused, Tired, Depressed
- **Tired eyes**: Tired, Depressed
- **Wide eyes**: Scared, Amazed, Interested
- **Raised eyebrows**: Startled, Skeptical, Curious
- **Tearful**: Sad, Hurt, Thankful

### Face and jaw

- **Hot or flushed face**: Mad, Embarrassed, Ashamed
- **Blushing**: Embarrassed, Exposed, Aroused
- **Scrunched-up face**: Repelled, Annoyed, Confused
- **Clenched jaw**: Mad, Resentful, Stressed
- **Smiling**: Happy, Playful, Proud

### Chest

- **Racing heart**: Scared, Mad, Excited
- **Tight chest**: Anxious, Overwhelmed, Resentful
- **Hollow feeling in your chest**: Lonely, Hurt, Empty
- **Warmth in your chest**: Loving, Thankful, Content

### Breath and throat

- **Shallow breathing**: Anxious, Stressed
- **Slow, deep breathing**: Peaceful, Content
- **Sharp intake of breath**: Startled, Scared
- **Lump in your throat**: Hurt, Grief, Thankful

### Stomach

- **Knot in your stomach**: Anxious, Guilty, Betrayed
- **Queasy stomach**: Repelled, Anxious
- **Butterflies in your stomach**: Nervous, Excited

### Arms and hands

Shortened from "Shoulders, arms and hands", which didn't fit its centre cell on phones (spec v2 §3.2). Tense shoulders stays in this area.

- **Tense shoulders**: Stressed, Anxious, Frustrated
- **Heavy arms and legs**: Tired, Depressed
- **Clenched fists**: Mad, Frustrated
- **Sweaty palms**: Nervous, Excited
- **Cold hands**: Scared, Nervous
- **Trembling or shaky**: Scared, Furious, Excited

### Whole body

- **Restless, need to move**: Anxious, Bored, Eager
- **Fidgety**: Nervous, Bored
- **Frozen or very still**: Scared, Shocked, Powerless
- **Low energy**: Depressed, Apathetic
- **Numbness**: Numb, Empty, Shocked
- **Light, floaty body**: Free, Joyful
- **Buzzing energy**: Excited, Inspired, Anxious
- **Relaxed muscles**: Peaceful, Trusting

### Posture and urges

- **Slumped posture**: Depressed, Inadequate, Tired
- **Open, upright posture**: Confident, Accepted
- **Urge to turn away**: Repelled, Withdrawn, Ashamed
- **Urge to shrink or hide**: Insecure, Ashamed, Humiliated
