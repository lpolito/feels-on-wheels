# Small World of Words: which words people link

Used in the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md)'s research and its synonym flag (`scripts/wheel-words/`).

## What it is

- Simon De Deyne, Danielle J. Navarro, Amy Perfors, Marc Brysbaert and Gert Storms, "The 'Small World of Words' English word association norms for over 12,000 cue words", *Behavior Research Methods* 51(3), 987–1006, 2019. [doi:10.3758/s13428-018-1115-7](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-018-1115-7). Data: SWOW-EN18, [smallworldofwords.org](https://smallworldofwords.org/en/project/research).
- **What it is:** the words that come to mind for over 12,000 cue words, from more than 90,000 people, with how strongly each pair is linked.
- Downloaded and used directly (Lucas downloaded it on 2026-11-20).

## Usage terms

CC BY-NC-ND; the download asks for a name and email. The data stays out of the repo, and nothing is transcribed from it.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **A flag, not a verdict:** links can show a word that seems out of place, but other meanings skew them (mad also means crazy), and they can't say one word is a kind of another.
2. **Synonym candidates** are a word's strongest links to other feeling words, when the link runs both ways.
