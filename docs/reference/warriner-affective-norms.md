# Warriner, Kuperman and Brysbaert: how pleasant a word is

The second list the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md)'s pleasantness check uses (`scripts/wheel-words/`).

## What it is

- Amy Beth Warriner, Victor Kuperman and Marc Brysbaert, "Norms of valence, arousal, and dominance for 13,915 English lemmas", *Behavior Research Methods* 45(4), 1191–1207, 2013. [doi:10.3758/s13428-012-0314-x](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-012-0314-x).
- **What it is:** about 13,900 English words, each rated by US raters on Mechanical Turk (about 20 per word, not checked against the full text) from 1 to 9 for pleasantness, energy, and control.
- Downloaded from the journal's supplement and used directly.

## Usage terms

No terms found. The data stays out of the repo; scripts read it from a local download.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **A second human-rated list,** so a word fails only when both lists that score it agree.
