# Goetz, Keltner and Simon-Thomas: compassion

The research behind the body wheel's **Fix things** covering wanting to help someone you care about, in the [body wheel rework](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-feels-on-wheels-body-wheel-rework-design.md).

## What it is

- Jennifer L. Goetz, Dacher Keltner and Emiliana Simon-Thomas, "Compassion: An evolutionary analysis and empirical review", *Psychological Bulletin* 136(3), 351–374, 2010. [doi:10.1037/a0018807](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018807). Open access: [PMC2864937](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2864937/).
- **The idea:** compassion is the feeling that comes from seeing someone suffer, and what sets it apart is the desire to help. The review argues it evolved to protect the vulnerable and those who suffer.
- Taken from the abstract and secondary summaries, read 2026-11-16; the full text wasn't read.

## Usage terms

Published research. We use the idea, not its text.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Wanting to help is an urge,** the defining one for compassion. It sits with guilt's urge to repair as one urge, wanting to fix things: one for your own mistake, one for someone else's trouble.
2. **Compassion isn't a word on the feelings wheel,** so the body wheel uses its closest words, Loving and Worried.
