# Trampe, Quoidbach and Taquet: feelings in daily life

Evidence for which core people would tap, in the [opinionated feelings wheel](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-feels-on-wheels-opinionated-wheel-design.md).

## What it is

- Debra Trampe, Jordi Quoidbach and Maxime Taquet, "Emotions in everyday life", *PLOS ONE* 10(12), e0145450, 2015. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0145450](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0145450). Open access.
- **What they did:** 11,572 people recorded which feelings they had, by phone, through the day: 65,721 reports.
- **What we used:** which feelings people report together, from the paper's supplementary table: people who feel the word usually also feel the core, so the core is the door.
- The paper and its supplementary table were read during the opinionated wheel's research (2026-11-22). The correlations are small, since people tick few feelings per report, so they're compared with each other, not with zero.

## Usage terms

Open access (CC BY). We use its findings, not its data.

## Takeaways for this project

1. **Embarrassed and Guilty under Bad:** in daily reports they come with each other and with fear, almost never with sadness.
2. **Grateful under Happy:** gratitude comes with joy five times as strongly as with love.
